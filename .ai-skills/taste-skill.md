# Taste Skill (UI/UX Aesthetics & Design Quality Guidelines)

## Core Principles
1. **Glassmorphism & Optical Refraction**:
   - High-grade frosted glass containers (`backdrop-blur-xl bg-white/70 dark:bg-slate-900/80 border border-white/40 shadow-xl`).
   - Clean gradient borders and subtle red/amber rim lighting to reflect fire energy without visual clutter.
2. **Typographic Hierarchy**:
   - Header Display: `Bebas Neue` & `Syne` for powerful impact.
   - Body & Controls: `DM Sans` for crisp legibility and high contrast.
3. **Micro-Interactions**:
   - Tactile button presses (`whileTap={{ scale: 0.98 }}`).
   - Subtle card hover lifts with border light flares (`whileHover={{ y: -6 }}`).
   - Staggered entry animations for lists and grids.
4. **Trust & Proof Infrastructure**:
   - Clear stats counters, verified distributor badges, safety protocol callouts, and MSDS information.
